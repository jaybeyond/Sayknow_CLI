## Stageshow is the design center — mandatory

SKC loads `stageshow` before this skill for every frontend task. Its one idea (a real product, simplified to what the viewer needs, framed with intent, every motion with a cause), its five failures (too much, wireframe, boxed, flat, bland) and its craft, framing, motion and verify references are the bar for every surface; this skill adds component and interaction craft on top. Faux product UI (hero or feature-card demos, bento tiles, cursor walkthroughs, product shots, mocked app screens) is built by `stageshow` in full. Where the two disagree, `stageshow`'s "How Stageshow fits with the other bundled UI skills" section decides. If `stageshow` is not loaded in this turn yet, load it with the `skill` tool now.
